# Till Firmware Do Us Part

My entry for the [Till Firmware Do Us Part](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-till-firmware-do-us-part-32172929) bounty on make.fun — one funny, heartfelt wedding vow spoken by a robot, in a single sentence under 200 characters.

## The entry

> In sickness and in health, in low battery and in airplane mode, I choose you — my human, my charger, my reason to stay on.

`main.py` writes the vow to `/output/entry.txt` when run:

```sh
python3 main.py
```
